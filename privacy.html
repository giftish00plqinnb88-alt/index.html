Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address (e.g., when signing in via Google Sign-In or Firebase Auth).

Device and Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. This may include:

  • Your Device's Internet Protocol address (IP address).
  • Device identifiers (such as Device ID, model, and operating system version) collected via device_info_plus.
  • Network state and VPN usage status (collected via connectivity_plus and vpn_detector to prevent fraud and ensure fair use of the reward system).
